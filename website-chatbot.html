The problem

Where website enquiries go cold

Most people who land on your site leave without telling you they were there. They have one question, do you cover my area, do you take on this kind of job, how soon could you start, and the answer isn't on the page they're reading. There's a contact form, but filling one in means waiting until tomorrow for a reply, so they go back to the search results and open the next tab instead. You never see that enquiry, because it never became one. The traffic reports as a visit and nothing else.

Process

How we build an AI chatbot for your website

  1. 01

    We start from your real questions

    We go through what people actually ask you before they buy, and which of those answers are already somewhere on your site.

  2. 02

    You decide what it handles

    What it should answer, what it should never touch, and what happens when someone is ready to act. That scope is the build.

  3. 03

    We build it and put it live

    We handle installation. You don't need a developer, and you don't need to rebuild anything you already have.

  4. 04

    We read what visitors asked and tighten it

    After launch we go through how it's handling real questions and adjust the answers until the common ones are covered well.

Use cases

What we build it to do

  • Answer the questions that decide a sale

    Do you cover my area, do you take on this kind of job, how soon could you start.

  • Qualify before you spend time

    Ask the two or three things you need to know before a job is worth quoting.

  • Take details when someone's ready

    Name, contact and what they need, passed to you however you want to receive it.

  • Point people to the right page

    Send a visitor to the service, the example or the form that actually answers them.

  • Cover the hours nobody's watching

    Evenings and weekends, when your site still gets traffic and nobody is at a desk.

  • Hand over when it should

    Some questions shouldn't be answered by software. You decide which, and it passes those to you.
