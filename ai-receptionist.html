The problem

What a missed call actually costs

Most local businesses lose work at the phone, not the pitch. You're on a job, driving, or with a customer, and the call goes to voicemail. Most callers don't leave one, they ring the next business on Google. Evenings and weekends are worse, because that's when people have time to call. Hiring someone to cover every hour is out of reach for most, so the phone keeps ringing out. That's the gap an AI receptionist for small business owners fills: it answers on the first ring, at 9pm on a Sunday, for every caller at once.

Process

How an AI answering service for business works

  1. 01

    We learn how your phone is answered today

    We listen to how you and your team answer, what callers ask most, and which questions actually decide whether someone books.

  2. 02

    We write what it can and cannot say

    Your greeting, the questions it asks, and the line it never crosses: it transfers to a person only when the caller asks, and only inside your working hours.

  3. 03

    We build it on Vapi or Retell

    We use both and choose per project, based on which handles your call patterns, languages and integrations better. You are not locked into whichever one we prefer.

  4. 04

    We listen to real calls and tune it

    It goes live taking a share of your calls while you hear the recordings, and we adjust the wording until it sounds like your business.

Use cases

What the receptionist handles

  • New enquiry calls

    Takes the caller's name, number, job and address, and puts it straight into your system.

  • Booking and rescheduling

    Offers real slots from your calendar and confirms by text.

  • Your most common questions

    Opening hours, areas covered, what you do and don't take on, answered the same way every time.

  • Out-of-hours and weekend calls

    Explains that the team isn't available, takes the enquiry in full, and sends it to you for the morning.

  • Overflow when the line is busy

    Picks up the calls your team can't reach, so nobody hears a busy tone.

  • Transfers when asked

    If the caller asks for a person and it's inside working hours, it puts them through.

Appointment booking

Booking appointments while you work

It doesn't just take a message. It reads your live calendar, offers the slots you actually have, and writes the booking in while the caller is still on the phone.

  • Real availability

    Only slots that are free in your calendar, so two customers never get the same time.

  • Confirmation and reminders

    A confirmation straight away and a reminder before the day, which is what cuts no-shows.

  • Rescheduling without phone tag

    If someone needs to move, they can do it on the call or by replying to the reminder.

Missed-call text back

Every missed call gets a text back

If a call isn't answered for any reason, the caller gets a text within seconds asking what they need. Most people reply to a text when they wouldn't leave a voicemail, so the enquiry survives instead of going to the next business on the list.

  • Sent in seconds

    Fast enough that the caller is still thinking about you rather than dialling someone else.

  • A real conversation

    They can reply with what they need and book from the text thread, not just leave a name.

  • Nothing lost at busy times

    Works when the line is engaged, when you're on a job, and outside hours.
