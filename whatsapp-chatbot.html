The problem

When the business runs through your personal phone

WhatsApp is where your customers already are, which is why it works and why it doesn't. The same phone that holds your family chats holds every enquiry, every price question and every "are you still coming?". They arrive while you're on a job, at dinner, on a Sunday, and the blue ticks have already told the customer you've seen it. Answer late and you look slow. Answer straight away and you never actually stop working. Handing the phone to someone else means handing over your personal messages with it.

Process

How we build a WhatsApp chatbot for business

  1. 01

    We start from the messages you already get

    We go through what customers actually send you, and which of those you answer the same way every time.

  2. 02

    You decide what it handles

    What it should answer, what it should never touch, and what happens when a message needs you. That scope is the build.

  3. 03

    We set up WhatsApp for business use

    We handle the setup, including which number it runs on, which is one of the first things we settle with you.

  4. 04

    We read the replies and tighten it

    After launch we go through how it's handling real messages and adjust the answers until the common ones are covered well.

Use cases

What we build it to answer

  • The price question, answered in seconds

    What it costs to do the thing they're asking about, answered the way you'd answer it, without the conversation stalling until you're free.

  • Whether you cover them

    Areas served, availability, and what you do or don't take on.

  • Questions that arrive at eleven at night

    Someone messaging after hours gets a real answer instead of two blue ticks.

  • The same five questions, every week

    The ones you've typed out a hundred times. It handles them so you don't.

  • Collect what you'd need to quote

    Takes the details you'd have to ask for anyway, so the job arrives ready to price.

  • Hand over when it should

    Some messages shouldn't be answered by software. You decide which, and it passes those to you.
