The problem

Where local businesses lose customers

Most local businesses don't lose customers to competitors. They lose them to missed calls, late replies and forgotten follow-ups. A caller who reaches voicemail rings the next business on Google. A website enquiry answered the next morning is already booked elsewhere. Hiring more staff to cover every hour is expensive, so the gaps stay open.

Process

How it works

  1. 01

    Find where the work leaks

    We go through your phone, website, WhatsApp and inbox and count what comes in, what gets answered, and what goes cold.

  2. 02

    Pick one job to automate

    We start with the single task costing you the most work, and write down what it should do and when it should hand over to you.

  3. 03

    Build it on your own tools

    We connect it to the calendar, phone and CRM you already use, then test it against real enquiries from the last few months, not made-up examples.

  4. 04

    Run it beside you first

    It handles a small share of the real volume while you watch every message, and we tune the wording until you'd have sent it yourself.

Use cases

What we automate for local businesses

  • AI receptionist

    Answers every call, day or night, takes the details you need and puts the job straight into your calendar.

  • Website chatbot

    Replies to questions on your website the moment they're asked, so nobody leaves because they waited.

  • WhatsApp chatbot

    Answers WhatsApp messages the way a good member of staff would, and passes anything unusual to you.

  • Missed-call text back

    If a call goes unanswered, the caller gets a text within seconds asking what they need, before they ring someone else.

  • Appointment booking and reminders

    Customers pick a slot themselves, and a reminder goes out before the day so fewer people forget.

  • Lead follow-up and nurture

    Every enquiry gets a reply, then a polite nudge a few days later if they've gone quiet.

  • Google review requests after a job

    Asks happy customers for a review at the moment the work is fresh, so your rating climbs without you chasing.

  • Quote and estimate requests

    Collects what you need to price a job, sends the quote out, and tells you when it's been opened.

  • Invoice and payment chasing

    Sends the invoice, then chases it politely until it's paid, so you're not the one asking twice.

  • CRM data entry and syncing

    New enquiries land in your system with the details already filled in, so nothing gets typed twice or lost in a notebook.
