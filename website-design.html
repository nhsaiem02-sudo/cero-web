The problem

When the site gets visits but no enquiries

Plenty of small business sites get found. The problem starts after that. A visitor lands and can't tell at a glance what you do, whether you work in their area, or why you rather than the next result. The page is slow on a phone, the things that matter are buried, and the only way to act on it is a contact form at the bottom. The analytics look healthy. The work doesn't arrive. A site that gets visits and produces nothing costs more than no site at all, because it wastes every visitor who was already interested enough to land on it.

Process

How we approach website design for small business

  1. 01

    We start from what you want the site to do

    Which enquiries are worth having, which ones waste your time, and what a visitor has to see before they'll get in touch.

  2. 02

    New build or upgrade, decided first

    A site from scratch and an improvement to the one you have are different pieces of work. We look at what you already have before assuming you need to start again.

  3. 03

    Built to be read, by people and by machines

    Fast on a phone, clear about what you do, and structured so Google and the AI assistants your customers now search with can find and recommend you.

  4. 04

    Live, then adjusted on what actually happens

    Once it's up we go through how it's performing with you and change what's getting in the way.

Use cases

What we build

  • A new site from scratch

    For a business starting out, or replacing one that can't be salvaged. Domain, hosting and any booking or payment setup are part of the conversation.

  • An upgrade to the site you have

    Often the cheaper answer. No new domain or hosting to buy, just access to what's already there.

  • Pages that say what you actually do

    What you offer, where you work and who you're for, visible immediately rather than three clicks in.

  • Fast on a phone

    Most of your visitors are on one, and a slow page loses them before it finishes loading.

  • Readable to AI assistants

    Structured so Google and the assistants people now search with can find you and recommend you.

  • An obvious way to get in touch

    On every page, not a form at the bottom of one.
